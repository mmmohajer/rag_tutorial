# Where can I learn more about request verbs and headers in your website?

You can explore these **free articles and tips** on Moh’s website:

- **[Five request verbs — article](https://tipsbymoh.tech/blog/five-request-verbs)**  
  Covers GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, and DELETE using a blog API, including the `/delete` path mistake.

- **[Request headers — article](https://tipsbymoh.tech/blog/request-headers)**  
  Covers Content-Type, Accept, and Authorization, including the token-in-the-URL mistake.

- **[Token in the header — article](https://tipsbymoh.tech/blog/token-in-the-header)**  
  Focuses on why the bearer token belongs in Authorization rather than the blog’s JSON body.

- **[Five request verbs — video tip and quiz](https://tipsbymoh.tech/tips/five-request-verbs)**  
  A short walkthrough with a quiz to check your understanding.
